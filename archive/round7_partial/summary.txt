Design
----------------------------------------
Prompt ensemble: single model (llama-3.1-8b-instant), three analytical persona prompts (value, sentiment, fundamentals). No inter-persona interaction. Disagreement is induced by analytical perspective — each persona reads the filing once in isolation. This design holds model architecture constant so that measured disagreement reflects filing content and analytical framing, not model-to-model differences.

Limitations and open questions
----------------------------------------
1. PROMPT-ENSEMBLE DESIGN: all three analytical personas use the same model (llama-3.1-8b-instant). Cross-persona disagreement reflects analytical framing differences, not model-architecture differences. This is intentional — per advisor guidance (June 2026), it isolates the effect of analytical perspective.
2. FAMILIARITY CONFOUND: the model may have stronger priors for large-cap firms (AAPL, MSFT, JPM) from pre-training, which could suppress persona disagreement regardless of filing content. The anonymization test (output/anonymization_test.csv) was run on the 11-firm pilot subset; results were inconclusive. At n=30, anonymization_delta becomes a firm-level control variable for regression analysis.
3. SAMPLE SIZE: n=8 firms, 15 filing-observations in this run. Proxy correlations are computed on the subset with available CRSP/Compustat data. All tier gradient results should be treated as measure-validation evidence, not the definitive return-test sample (which requires many more firms and time-series depth).
4. EMBEDDING MEASURE: neural embeddings (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) are new to this run. Interpret with caution until validated on a larger sample. If embedding_disagreement shows no variation on the 30-firm expansion, investigate the embedding pipeline.
5. PROXY ASYNCHRONY: market_cap, analyst_count, and idiosyncratic_volatility are measured as of today, not at the filing date. For volatile small-caps this can differ substantially from the filing-date value.
6. MULTIPLE TESTING: Bonferroni correction applied with α=0.05/n_tests across all Spearman tests. No correction applied to the trading volume regression (separate pre-specified test).
7. CAUSAL IDENTIFICATION: cross-sectional Spearman ρ does not establish direction of causality. Higher LLM disagreement → lower market cap is equally consistent with disagreement being a cause (complexity discount) or an effect (small firms are less covered and more ambiguous).
