Coverage diagnostic — what the fixed word cap did to the sample
========================================================================

Retired design: MAX_WORDS_PER_FILING = 2000, applied to the concatenated
narrative sections after extraction.
Current design: MD&A and Risk Factors extracted complete; comparability enforced
at the chunk level (1200 words per window).

Filings in sample: 59
Filings truncated by the old cap: 59 of 59

  mid    n=20  median section = 11,750 words | read: mean 17.9%, median 17.0%, min 7.2%

THE PROBLEM — coverage was correlated with the independent variable
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  tier rank (large=3) x fraction read          n=59  rho=-0.415  p=0.0011
  tier rank (large=3) x complete section words n=59  rho=+0.415  p=0.0011

  Larger firms file longer narrative sections, so a fixed cap read a
  smaller share of them.  'How much of the filing the model read' was
  therefore a function of firm size — the variable the hypothesis is
  about — and any tier gradient in disagreement was confounded with a
  tier gradient in coverage.  Under the current design every filing is
  read in full, so this correlation is undefined by construction.
