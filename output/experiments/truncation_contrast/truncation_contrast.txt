Budget rules
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  inclusive: every filing contributes min(k, its window count) windows;
             a filing with fewer than k windows contributes all of them.
  strict   : only filings with >= k windows contribute. Drops are not
             tier-neutral — short filings are disproportionately small-cap.
  k = 1    : the pre-registered usability gate is evaluated once on each
             filing's COMPLETE MD&A, not inside the budget, so k = 1 is
             analysable. Within-filing SD is undefined at k = 1 and is
             reported as NaN, never 0. The count of filings that would
             fail a within-budget gate is in the diagnostics frame.
