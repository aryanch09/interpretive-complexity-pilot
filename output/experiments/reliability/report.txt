How to read these numbers
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Split-half is the measurement-reliability estimate: it asks whether two
  disjoint halves of the SAME filing agree. Test-retest asks a different
  and harder question — whether disagreement is a stable property of the
  FIRM across two different documents — so it is attenuated by genuine
  cross-document variation and is a lower bound, not a reliability
  coefficient. A high split-half with a low test-retest would mean the
  measure is reliable but document-specific, which is itself a finding and
  would need saying plainly rather than hiding.
