Reliability extrapolation — what would this measure need?
==========================================================================

observed split-half r (half-length filing mean) : +0.136
Spearman-Brown reliability of the FULL mean     : +0.239
median MD&A windows per filing                 : 5

Windows per filing required to reach a target reliability:
    target   windows needed   vs current
      0.60               24         4.8x
      0.70               37         7.4x
      0.80               64        12.7x
      0.90              143        28.6x

At 1200 words per window, the median filing currently supplies
~6,000 words of MD&A. Reaching the conventional 0.70 threshold would
need ~37 windows, i.e. ~44,550 words — far more MD&A than
a typical filing contains.

READING: the shortfall is not a sampling accident that a bigger firm
sample would fix. Filing-level aggregation of this measure is limited by
how much narrative a filing HAS. Raising reliability requires a different
estimator — pooling across filings or years, modelling at the window level
rather than averaging to the filing, or a lens set that disagrees more
systematically — not more firms.
