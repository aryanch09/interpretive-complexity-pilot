How to read this
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  What must hold for the paper's claim to be encoder-independent is that the
  TIER ORDERING and the trend test agree across encoders. High window-level
  rank correlation is reassuring but not sufficient, and a moderate one is
  not fatal: different encoders can disagree on individual windows while
  agreeing on firm-level aggregates, which is the level the hypothesis is
  about. If the ordering flips under another encoder, say so plainly — that
  would mean the result is an encoder artifact.
