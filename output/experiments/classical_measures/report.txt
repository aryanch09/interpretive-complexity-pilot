How to read this
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A level shift under truncation is unsurprising on its own. What matters
  for a size regression is whether the distortion is TIER-DEPENDENT, and
  whether it moves the measured gradient. Where it does, truncation is not
  merely adding noise; it is changing the cross-sectional answer, for
  measures this literature has used for years and that have nothing to do
  with language models.

  type_token_ratio is reported for completeness and should NOT be read as
  a finding: TTR falls mechanically as text lengthens, so any difference
  between a 2,000-word and a complete arm is partly arithmetic.
